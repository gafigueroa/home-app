Privacy policy

Home is a personal app, used only by its author, with their own Google account. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses: with the author's permission (Google sign-in), their Gmail and Google Calendar, to answer their questions, write email drafts and manage calendar events when they ask.

Where data goes: Google sign-in tokens are kept only on the author's own computer. Email and calendar content is read when needed to answer a request and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Requests are answered by Anthropic's Claude, under Anthropic's terms.

Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
